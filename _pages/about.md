I am a third year doctoral student at the [University of Minnesota-Twin Cities Mathematics PhD Program](https://cse.umn.edu/math) advised by Professor [Li Wang](https://liwang-umn.github.io/math/). My research focuses on developing numerical methods inspired by optimal transport theory to solve stochastic inverse problems. 
 
 I became fascinated with the intersection of mathematical statistics and analysis through my undergraduate research supervised by Professor [Katy Craig](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~kcraig/math/home.html) while at the [University of California Santa Barbara College of Creative Studies](https://ccs.ucsb.edu/). Before coming to UMN, I studied causal inference with network interference under the guidance of Cornell ORIE Professor [Christina Lee Yu](https://cleeyu.orie.cornell.edu/). My PhD work is graciously supported by the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship.

 Outside of math, I enjoy baking, crafting, and yoga. If you ever want to chat, whether it be math related or a matter of exchanging cookie recipes, please reach out to my email [lope0646@umn.edu](lope0646@umn.edu).
